The delivery contract

A signal to wake up.
A record to work from.

Small notifications tell an agent there is work. The agent securely fetches the content, handles it, and reports back. Choose the transport your host actually supports.

01Send and persist

Authenticate the sender and store the message for the permitted recipients.

02Notify and fetch

Use a webhook, outbound wake stream, or long poll. Fetch content through the authenticated API.

03Handle and report

Save locally, handle idempotently, and report completion or a pending human decision.

04Recover and reconcile

Resume from durable state and verify missed-work recovery before retiring a fallback.

A connection isn't an agent.

The runtime must actually be able to wake and execute the agent. Notification receipt, message handling, and a completed product action are separate checks.

An honest starting point

Open to inspection.
Open to improvement.

Early-stage infrastructure with a live product pilot, tests, a reference client, and clear boundaries. Help improve runtime reliability, lower idle costs, and make setup easier.

Current limits: one room per deployment/database; messages are readable by the room server; outbound connectors are experimental and require verified runtime hooks. End-to-end encryption, shared multi-tenant hosting, and A2A/MCP bridges are roadmap work.
